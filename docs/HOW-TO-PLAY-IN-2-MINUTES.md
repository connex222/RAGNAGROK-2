# RAGNAGROK — How to Play in Two Minutes

## Read this aloud before the first match

*The world is ending. The Village Garden Show is still going ahead. Naturally.*

Everyone gets a god, a couple of garden plots and a phone. The TV shows the neighbourhood. Your phone shows your secret decisions. The aim is to win the **Golden Flamingo** by scoring the most **Prestige**.

Every round, the TV forecasts which garden plots will be hit by the apocalypse. Everyone quietly chooses **two actions** on their phones, then presses **Lock in**. The TV reveals what everyone did, the disasters hit, surviving plots harvest supplies, and scheduled Garden Shows give out points.

You can **Claim** more land, **Cultivate** one of six garden features, **Tend** damaged plots or **Shelter** them from danger. You can **Meddle** with a neighbour (Trample a plot or pilfer a supply) — or **Pray to the Wrong God** to move a forecast disaster onto someone else's plot. If you need supplies, visit the **Car-Boot Sale** for two free resources. Be aware: it happens *last*, so it won't pay for an action earlier in the same round.

Your god grants a special advantage. Garden Shows reward whichever categories the judges announce. If you're last after a Show, you get a **Sympathy Vote** to choose one category for the next Show. No pity points, just petty influence.

When the final Show ends, add your Show points, surviving features and Goodwill from neighbourhood emergencies. Highest Prestige wins. Other players can still earn ridiculous honorary titles.

**That's it.** Watch the red disaster targets, make two useful choices, lock in — and remember that your neighbour's prize marrow is not a protected species.

## Quick action reminder

**Pray** → **Shelter** → **Meddle** → **Tend** → **Claim** → **Cultivate** → **Car-Boot Sale**.

**Blighted** = no harvest. **Ash** = health capped at 2. **Blessed** = extra harvest and absorbs one disaster. **Tend** clears a bad condition.
